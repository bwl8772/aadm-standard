# Customer story — AI task assistant (example)

## Title

Capture follow-ups during meetings without switching tools.

## Persona

**Jordan**, a product manager juggling multiple stakeholder meetings.

## Situation

Action items live in scattered notes. Engineers lose context; deadlines slip.

## Need

A single place to capture tasks with enough structure that engineering can plan without another clarification round.

## Value

Fewer dropped commitments; faster cycle from discussion to backlog-ready items.
