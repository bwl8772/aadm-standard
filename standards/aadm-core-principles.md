# AADM core principles (normative summary)

Adopters may incorporate these statements into internal standards. They describe **intent**, not a particular toolchain.

1. **Architecture intent is explicit and revisable** before broad implementation.  
2. **Contracts** at system boundaries are owned, versioned, and reviewed.  
3. **Authorization** is enforced where domain meaning is interpreted—not only at the outermost UI.  
4. **Automation—including AI agents—operates within declared scopes** and does not substitute for human accountability on high-risk decisions.  
5. **Work is mapped to a layered model** so reviews target the correct concerns.  
6. **Observability and evidence** match the strength of claims made about correctness or compliance.  
7. **Security and privacy constraints** are properties of the design, not optional stretch goals.
