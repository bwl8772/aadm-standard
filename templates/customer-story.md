# Customer story

_A single sentence from the customer or user’s point of view. Use it to anchor PRD-lite, BIS-lite, and acceptance criteria._

## Story format

> **As a** [role], **I want to** [action or capability], **so that** [business or user outcome].

---

## Fields

| Piece | Guidance |
|-------|-----------|
| **Role** | Specific persona (not “the user”) |
| **Action / capability** | What they can do in the product—verb-led |
| **Outcome** | Benefit or risk reduced; observable or credible |

## Example (fictional)

**As a** freelance designer **working across several client workspaces**, **I want to** export my weekly timesheet as CSV **so that** I can invoice without manually copying hours from the app.

_Context:_ Currently they screenshot the summary; mistakes cause billing disputes.
